Need help with your garden, account, backup, or a community safety concern?

Contact us

Email 330891184l@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Garden Story version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send passwords, Apple authorisation codes, private keys or sensitive photos.

You can also open Garden Story and choose Account → Send Feedback.

Cloud backup and restore

Sign in with the same Apple account, then use Account → Back Up Now. Restore is intended for an empty Garden Story installation. Keep the app open on a reliable connection until the operation finishes.

Community safety

Use Report on a post or comment to send it for review. Use Block gardener to hide interaction between your accounts. For an urgent concern that cannot be reported in the app, email us with a description and the relevant account or post details.

Delete your account

In Garden Story, open Account → Delete Account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and associated cloud data, including uploaded photos and community activity. Local plants and photos remain on the device unless you delete them or remove the app.

Can’t access the app?
Email us from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a data request.
