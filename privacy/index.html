Garden Story helps you keep a private plant journal, back it up, and choose what to share with other gardeners.

Information we collect

Account information

When you use Sign in with Apple, Garden Story and its authentication provider receive an account identifier and may receive your email address or Apple private relay address. We use this information to authenticate you and associate your cloud data with your account.

Garden and profile content

If you use cloud backup or community features, we store information you provide, which may include your garden name, profile photo, plants, categories, care plans, tasks, journal entries, lifecycle events, notes and dates.

Photos and community activity

We store photos you choose to back up or publish, along with stories, comments and their reply relationships, likes, saves, follows, reports and block relationships needed to operate the community. Garden plants, their journals and private backups stay private. Only stories and journal photos you explicitly publish, and their comments, can be viewed by other signed-in users. Your garden name and optional profile photo are visible with your public contributions and public garden. Other users may copy or screenshot public content; deleting it from Garden Story cannot remove copies held by others.

Feedback and support

We store feedback, catalogue corrections, safety and copyright reports, moderation-review requests and messages you submit so that we can respond and investigate. Reports may include contact details, evidence of ownership, and the content involved. Reports are not public posts. We may share information needed to investigate or respond with affected parties, service providers or authorities where appropriate and lawful; avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.

Photo controls and preferences

Camera and photo-library access is used when you choose a photo. Profile photos are cropped and resized for display. You can replace or remove your avatar in Account → Edit profile. Optional story watermarks are added to the published copy, using your garden name and the Garden Story mark; your original photo is not changed. The app remembers your watermark preference and the version of community terms accepted for your account on this device.

Location

If you permit location access, Garden Story uses latitude on your device to determine your hemisphere for seasonal care guidance. Garden Story does not upload or retain your location coordinates.

Service records

Our hosting provider may process limited technical records, such as network requests, timestamps and error information, to secure and operate the service. Garden Story does not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and keep them secure.
  • Provide cloud backup, restore, catalogue and community features.
  • Display content you deliberately make public.
  • Process reports, enforce community rules and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to feedback and support requests.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and protect the service.

Service providers and disclosure

Garden Story uses Apple for authentication and Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions. These providers process information on our behalf to deliver the service. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or with your direction. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You control which stories or journal photos you publish. Your public garden displays your shared stories, rather than your private Garden records. You can edit or delete community posts, block gardeners, decline optional permissions, sign out, and remove local content using the app’s controls.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and cloud content while your account is active or as needed to provide and protect the service. You can delete your account from Account → Delete Account. This deletes the account and associated cloud content, including uploaded photos and community data. Plants and photos stored locally on your device remain until you remove the app or delete them. Limited records may remain temporarily where required for security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or until protected backups expire.

Children

Garden Story is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

Security and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process data in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

Changes

We may update this policy as Garden Story changes. We will update the effective date on this page and provide additional notice where required.
